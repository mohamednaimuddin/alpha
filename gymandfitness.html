Gym flooring questions

What type of rubber flooring is suitable for a gym?

The appropriate format depends on the training zones, equipment, expected impact, floor area and maintenance plan. Mats suit targeted equipment or weight areas, rolls provide broader continuous coverage, and modular tiles can simplify handling and replacement. Final thickness and construction should be confirmed for the actual use.

What is the difference between rubber tiles and rolls?

Tiles divide the floor into modular sections that can be handled and replaced individually. Rolls cover larger areas with fewer joins. The better option depends on room size, layout, subfloor, installation method and how the gym will be used.

How do I choose gym flooring thickness?

There is no single thickness for every gym. Selection should consider equipment weight, expected impact, training type, subfloor and product construction. Send Alpha Rubber the intended use and project details so available thickness options can be reviewed without assuming a specification.
