03 Buyer questions

Rubber flooring and mat FAQs

What products does Alpha Rubber provide?

Alpha Rubber provides rubber flooring rolls, gym mats, SBR and EPDM tiles, industrial matting, entrance mats, anti-fatigue and kitchen mats, stable and cow mats, plus selected specialist surface products for UAE projects.

What is the difference between EPDM and SBR rubber flooring?

EPDM and SBR are different rubber material families. On this website, both appear in tile and roll product ranges. Performance and construction vary by product, so colour, intended environment, thickness and technical requirements should be confirmed from the specific product documentation before selection.

Can rubber flooring help manage impact and noise?

Resilient rubber surfaces are commonly selected where cushioning and impact-sound control matter, but actual results depend on material, density, thickness, subfloor and installation. Request the relevant technical information if a project must meet a defined acoustic or impact rating.

Does Alpha Rubber serve projects across the UAE?

Alpha Rubber is based in the United Arab Emirates and accepts enquiries for UAE commercial and specialist projects. Exact delivery areas, lead times and installation availability should be confirmed with the team for each project location.

What information should I include in a quotation request?

Include the product or application, project location, estimated area or quantity, preferred dimensions, thickness, colour, finish and any known performance requirements. This helps the team identify the right product family and confirm which specifications are available.
