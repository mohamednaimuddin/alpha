03 Buyer questions

Gym floor mat questions

What are rubber gym floor mats used for?

Rubber gym floor mats provide targeted coverage in free-weight areas, beneath exercise equipment and in flexible workout zones. The suitable construction and thickness depend on the expected equipment, impact and subfloor.

How do I choose gym floor mat thickness?

Consider equipment weight, expected impact, training activity, subfloor and product construction. Alpha Rubber reviews the intended use before confirming available thickness options.

Are individual gym mats different from rubber flooring rolls?

Individual mats cover selected zones and can be handled separately. Rubber flooring rolls provide broader continuous coverage with fewer joins. Room layout, equipment and maintenance priorities help determine the suitable format.

Do you supply gym floor mats across the UAE?
